CYBER HYGIENE

Best Security Practices

Follow these essential protocols to safeguard your personal identity.

01

Enable Two-Factor (2FA/MFA)

Switch to hardware keys or app-based authenticator codes (TOTP) instead of standard SMS-based codes.

02

Zero-Trust Download Rule

Never run untrusted attachments (.exe, .zip, .apk) sent via WhatsApp, Telegram, or unknown emails.

03

Keep Patch Updates Active

Promptly update your OS, web browsers, and firewall security signatures to patch known zero-day vulnerabilities.

04

Isolate Public Wi-Fi Networks

Avoid logging into online bank accounts or entering credit card details while connected to open public hotspots.
